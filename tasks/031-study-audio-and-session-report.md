# Task 031 — Study, audio, and session report

## Goal

Turn the capture-to-quiz flow into a learn-practice-review loop.

## Allowed files

- `apps/web/app/capture-workspace.tsx`
- `apps/web/app/page.test.tsx`
- `apps/web/app/styles.css`
- `tasks/031-study-audio-and-session-report.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- The pre-game review shows definitions, examples, and audio controls.
- Words, incomplete sentences, and complete examples can be spoken with browser TTS.
- The final report shows score and percentage.
- The report distinguishes correct and incorrect answers.
- Each result can be expanded to review meaning, example, and pronunciation.
