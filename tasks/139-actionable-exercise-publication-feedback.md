# Task 139 — Actionable exercise publication feedback

## Goal

Replace generic session-publication failures with precise, recoverable learner guidance while
preserving the final verified-exercise gate.

## Acceptance criteria

- Distinguish authentication, expiration, invalid selection, and no-published-exercise failures.
- Name omitted words when final publication produces fewer than four exercises.
- Recommend replacing only omitted or missing items.
- Do not expose answers or internal provider details.
- Keep the final publication gate authoritative.
