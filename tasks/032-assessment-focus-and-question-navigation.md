# Task 032 — Assessment focus and question navigation

## Goal

Reduce answer leakage, fix audio interaction, and let learners review questions before finishing.

## Allowed files

- `apps/web/app/capture-workspace.tsx`
- `apps/web/app/page.test.tsx`
- `apps/web/app/styles.css`
- `tasks/032-assessment-focus-and-question-navigation.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- Selection cards show the term, word class, and pronunciation without definitions or examples.
- Gap audio pauses at the missing word and never says “blank”.
- Audio controls cannot select or visually mark another answer.
- Answered questions can be revisited before final submission.
- Previous answers and their feedback remain visible when revisiting.
