# Task 030 — Guided multiple-choice session

## Goal

Replace the single typed-answer dead end with a complete, lower-friction learning session.

## Allowed files

- `apps/web/app/capture-workspace.tsx`
- `apps/web/app/page.test.tsx`
- `apps/web/app/styles.css`
- `tasks/030-guided-multiple-choice-session.md`

## Acceptance criteria

- Every question displays a sentence gap and four alternatives.
- The learner receives immediate correct/incorrect feedback and an explanation.
- Incorrect answers do not end or reset the session.
- The learner advances through every selected word.
- A final score appears only after the last question.
