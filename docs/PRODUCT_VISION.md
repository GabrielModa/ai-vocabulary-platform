# Product vision

## Promise

Help learners turn useful English words into durable, usable knowledge through short contextual
sessions that feel like a premium game rather than schoolwork.

## Learner outcome

A mastered word can be recognized, understood in varied contexts, pronounced, heard, spelled,
recalled without hints, and used naturally after increasing delays. Seeing a definition is not
mastery.

## Initial hypothesis

The first release should validate one complete loop: select appropriate vocabulary, teach it in
context, retrieve it through varied prompts, provide immediate feedback, schedule later retrieval,
and show evidence-based progress. Story, conversation, social, and advanced game modes follow only
when that loop produces measurable retention.

## Success measures

- Retention at agreed 1-day, 7-day, and 30-day intervals
- Successful unhinted recall and transfer to unseen contexts
- Lesson completion without increased error or abandonment from overload
- Accessible task completion across supported devices and assistive technologies
- Sustainable latency, reliability, and cost per active learner

Engagement metrics are diagnostic, not the goal. Streaks, sessions, or XP cannot substitute for
learning outcomes.

## Product boundaries

The platform is not a dictionary, generic chatbot, isolated flashcard tool, exam credential, or
replacement for qualified language teaching. Launch ages, CEFR levels, interface languages, regions,
catalog size, and commercial model require product-owner approval before implementation.
