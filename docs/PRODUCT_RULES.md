# Product rules

1. Every learning interaction must elicit understanding or retrieval in context.
2. Do not present isolated definition memorization as the primary activity.
3. Introduce one meaningful difficulty at a time and adapt using demonstrated evidence.
4. Give immediate, specific, supportive feedback without hiding the correct model indefinitely.
5. Use multiple contexts, modalities, and directions of recall over time.
6. Never equate reward activity with mastery.
7. Do not punish learners for accessibility needs, offline use, time zones, illness, or missed days.
8. Ask for the minimum personal data required and make consent understandable and revocable.
9. Generated content must be validated, attributable to a version, and replaceable.
10. A degraded provider must not destroy progress or block already-downloaded learning.
11. One primary action per screen; secondary actions remain discoverable and accessible.
12. Experiments need a hypothesis, guardrails, exit criteria, and learning-quality measurement.

## Content acceptance

Content must be accurate, natural, level-appropriate, culturally respectful, safe for the approved
age range, and useful in realistic communication. Ambiguous word senses are taught separately.

## Release acceptance

No release may knowingly regress retention, accessibility, authorization, data integrity, or privacy
to improve cosmetic engagement metrics.
